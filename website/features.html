Product Capabilities

Complete, reliable finance management

Explore the tools and intuitive features built into KharchaFlow to keep your daily cash flow organized, accurate, and completely under your control.

1. Transaction Tracking

Precision Cash & Account Management

Cash & Account Sources

Separate cash-in-hand spending from bank account transactions. Set independent default sources for Income and Expenses.

1-Tap Quick Add

Launch the streamlined Quick Add dialog from the app or your home screen to record expenses with automatic category selection in seconds.

Zero Rounding Discrepancies

Built with exact integer subunit arithmetic, guaranteeing zero floating-point rounding errors in all balance calculations.

Live Search & Type Filters

Search instantly across transaction notes, categories, and amounts with instant filtering by All, Expense, or Income.

Accidental Delete Protection

Deleted an entry accidentally? KharchaFlow includes an instant notification allowing you to restore deleted records with one tap.

Global Multi-Currency

Seamlessly configure your preferred currency: INR (₹), USD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£), AED (د.إ), SGD (S$), CAD (C$), AUD (A$), and JPY (¥).

2. Budgeting & Insights

Financial Continuity & Analysis

Month Carry-Forward Ledger

Set an initial opening balance. Every month automatically calculates your surplus or deficit and carries forward the closing balance.

Bank-Grade PDF Statements

Generate clean PDF financial statements with running balance calculations directly on-device with zero external servers.

Day-Wise Calendar View

Inspect your daily financial timeline with interactive calendar distribution showing daily income and expense indicators.

3. Cloud Backup & Portability

Your Data, Backed Up Your Way

Optional Google Drive Backup

Back up your local database directly to your personal Google Drive account whenever you want with official authorization.

Private Cloud Folder

Backups are stored inside your private Google Drive app folder where only your personal installation of KharchaFlow can access them.

Confirmation-Guarded Restore

Restoring from a Google Drive backup requires explicit user confirmation to ensure your local records are never overwritten accidentally.

4. Recurring & Automation

Automate Bills, Salaries & EMIs

EMI & Salary Schedules

Schedule fixed monthly income, loan EMIs, and utility subscriptions with reliable automatic tracking.

Smart Evening Reminder

Configurable daily reminder that checks if any transactions were recorded today; if already logged, the notification is quietly suppressed.

Home Screen Widgets

Two useful widgets: a Financial Overview widget for balance and today's spending, and a Quick Add launcher widget.

5. Security & Customization

Privacy, Locking and Personalization

Biometric & PIN Lock

Lock your finances behind fingerprint/face unlock or PIN with recent-apps window privacy protection.

Effortless Personalization

Easily manage custom categories, default payment sources, reminders, and backup options from an uncluttered preferences screen.

Tactile & Responsive

Comfortable touch targets, fluid screen transitions, and instant feedback make every interaction feel natural on mobile.

Languages & Localization

Full localization for English, Hindi (हिंदी), and Marathi (मराठी) with native date and number formatting.

Built-In Update Checker

Integrated updater in Settings to detect new releases, verify download integrity, and install updates safely.

Open & Verifiable

100% open-source with no telemetry, marketing cookies, or third-party tracking libraries.
